    Expression is the median over replicates in the cysteine reactivity plots, and the published fold change for the whole proteome expression plots. The two are different statistics and can disagree.

    Whole proteome and RNA bars draw the published fold change, so each bar matches that protein's or gene's volcano position. The replicate points over them are supporting evidence, not what sets the bar. RNA error bars are ±1 lfcSE; whole proteome error bars are ±1 SEM of the plotted points. A replicate below detection is a bound rather than a measurement.

    In the cysteine reactivity ATP add-back plots, the box spans the interquartile range with the line at the median, and the whiskers reach the furthest replicates within 1.5× the IQR.
